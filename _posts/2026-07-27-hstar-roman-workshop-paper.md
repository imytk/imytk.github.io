I'm pleased to share that I've had a workshop paper accepted at the **Human State-Aware Robotics (H-STAR): From Multimodal Data to Human-Adaptive Behavior in HRI** workshop, held at **RO-MAN 2026** in Kitakyushu, Japan.

The paper touches on a few themes I'm increasingly interested in: how robots can read and reason about a person's internal state from noisy, multimodal signals, and how they might use that to adapt their own behaviour in a way that is genuinely responsive rather than merely reactive. It sits at the intersection of affective and social cognition, adaptive regulation, and human-robot interaction — very much in the spirit of the broader work I've been doing on adaptive and allostatic systems.

This is part of a larger paper I'm currently developing, so I'd love to talk to anyone thinking about similar problems. If any of this overlaps with your interests, please do [get in touch](mailto:imy@imytk.co.uk) — whether you'll be at RO-MAN or not.
