The **ALife Expo** is a public-facing, digital exhibition designed to open up the world of artificial life to everyone, with no background required. Growing out of the public expo I helped organise at ALIFE 2025, it guides visitors through the field's big ideas, such as emergence, self-organisation, evolution, and complexity from simplicity, in a set of accessible, self-contained "zones". It's part of an ongoing effort to make artificial life more engaging, playful, visible, and welcoming to curious minds outside the research community.
