The **What ALife! Podcast** is a show I host about artificial life and the people who study it. It's an outreach project at heart: part light-hearted conversation, part research round-up, and part deep-dive interview with scientists and practitioners across every career stage, all aimed at breaking down the complexities of ALife and making the field a little more accessible to everyone. You can listen below, or find it on Spotify, Amazon Music, and other platforms.
